Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can your agents work on our existing platform?

Yes. Our agents work directly in your systems, whether that is Zendesk, Salesforce Service Cloud, Five9, Talkdesk, TCN, Genesys Cloud or another platform. You don't need to buy new tools or change how your operation is set up.

Which platforms do you work with?

Our teams work on Zendesk (including DeepL translation), Salesforce Service Cloud, TCN, Webio, Five9 and Talkdesk, as well as Genesys Cloud, NICE CXone, Freshdesk, RingCentral Contact Center, Avaya and LivePerson.

Can you support customers in other languages?

Yes. On Zendesk we connect DeepL's AI translation so agents can help customers in their own language in real time, which suits businesses serving customers in more than one market.

Can you add AI to our contact centre without replacing our platform?

Yes. Our AfriCX technology adds AI that helps agents on live calls, quality checks on every conversation and clearer reporting on top of the platform you already have.

Do you use AI to replace agents?

No. People lead every customer conversation and own the outcome. We use AI to make them faster, more accurate and easier to measure.

Who looks after the technology your own teams use?

Our own IT team in Johannesburg. They run our secure in-office operation and also provide IT outsourcing to clients in the US and UK.
